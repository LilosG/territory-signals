# Decisions

Approved decisions, recorded 2026-11-18.

- **D1** Probate, court and obituary sources are not scraped; inheritance signals come from ATTOM recorder data or a licensed probate vendor (Phase 6). A permission request to capublicnotice/CNPA is sent separately by Mike.
- **D2** The treasurer-tax collector default list is ingested as a low-volume signal.
- **D3** STRO licenses are matched to parcels by point-in-polygon on latitude/longitude; only license id, tier, location and status are stored.
- **D4** Permit signal groups are mapped from the real distinct `APPROVAL_TYPE` values profiled in Phase 2, not guessed.
- **D5** The territory is defined by City planning areas: La Jolla, Pacific Beach, Mission Beach. ZIP is kept as an attribute. Bird Rock and La Jolla Shores stay inside La Jolla until Mike draws their boundaries.
- **D6** Fire hazard zones are added from a file Mike downloads by hand (Phase 2); never guessed.
- **D7** SB 79 and SB 9 eligibility is an in-house screen, stored with `is_estimate = true` and labeled "estimated eligibility". Official data is requested from the City or SANDAG.
- **D8** Parcel `DOCDATE` is shown only as "last recorded document date", never as a purchase date. Tenure and sales come from ATTOM.
- **D9** ATTOM is a required core source. Until the API key is added, the `SampleOwnerProvider` supplies labeled sample owner and sales records.
- **D10** Phase 5 outreach drafts are generated deterministically from recipe templates in `config/outreach_templates/`, using only scored component facts. AI-polished drafts may be added later on top of the same facts.
- **D11** California first-point-of-contact solicitation materials must show the agent's name and DRE license number; outreach cannot be approved for sending unless both are present in the client config.
- **D12** (2026-11-19, Phase 5E) Mission Beach shows 0 for SB 9 and both SB 79 bands, and that is correct, not a mapping gap. Its residential zones are `MBPD-*` planned districts, which the SB 9 screen leaves out (D7, BACKLOG.md); the nearest rail station is about 2.4 miles away and the nearest rapid-bus stop about 5 miles, so nothing is within 1/2 mile. The Trends takeaway now says so. Financial pressure is shown on the Recipes page as a labeled card ("Approval required"); it had been hidden by the `SENSITIVE_RECIPES` filter, which now applies only to Leads filters, cards and lists.
- **D13** (2026-11-19, Phase 5F) Intelligence layer v1. (1) **Provider-agnostic.** `pipelines/territory/llm/` has one interface (`complete(task, system, messages, json_schema)`) and three adapters: `openai_compatible` (openai SDK, any base URL: OpenRouter, xAI, OpenAI, Ollama), `anthropic` and a deterministic `fake` for tests. Switching is a config change only. (2) **The provider is a per-client choice**, because client data (parcel facts, permit text, aggregate counts) goes to that provider; each client's `config/clients/<id>.yaml` names it, its key env var and its monthly budget. (3) **OpenRouter** is used for Kaiser Team with `provider: {data_collection: "deny", require_parameters: true}` on every request, so only endpoints that do not retain or train on prompts serve it; cost is read from the response. Prices for other providers come from `config/llm_pricing.yaml`; a model with no price there is recorded as cost unknown, never guessed. (4) **Validation after every call.** Output is parsed with pydantic (one retry with the error appended, then `status = invalid` and nothing is shown), then checked by deterministic code: enums, quoted words found in the source text, every claim cites a record from the facts packet, every number appears in the packet, no address other than the subject's, no speculation about the owner beyond what a record states, and the newsletter paragraph passes the homeowner-text checker. Failures are stored as `rejected` with the reason and never shown. (5) **Facts packets never contain sensitive records** (tax default, ownership transfer, the distress recipes), because lead headlines appear on lists where those must never show (presentation rules). (6) **Permit intent runs in shadow mode**: it is stored and compared with the permit group map (`docs/reports/permit-intent-agreement.md`) but does not touch scores or recipes. (7) AI never sets scores, counts or eligibility; every AI text in the dashboard carries an "AI summary" tag and cites its records. A new provider or model must score at least 90% on `tests/llm_eval/` before a client config switches to it.
- **D14** (2026-11-19, Phase 5G) Pre-sale prep is decided by what the permit says, not only its type. (1) The seven electrical / plumbing / mechanical approval types stay `pre_sale_prep` *candidates*; `presale_rules` in `config/permit_signal_map.yaml` then reads the permit text (approval scope, else job description, else project title and scope) and keeps `pre_sale_prep` only for cosmetic or remodel scope (remodel, kitchen, bath, paint, flooring, windows/doors, roof, landscaping/hardscape, facade, addition, interior improvement). Weak room or fixture words do not count when systems-work words (repipe, drain, water heater, furnace, panel, meter, undergrounding) are present. (2) Like-for-like systems work, and any permit with no text, goes to a new `maintenance` group with weight 0. Tenant improvements, offices, cell sites and new buildings filed under these types go to `other`; solar and battery installs go to `solar_energy`. (3) Scoring model v0.4: only the permit group of the signals changed, no weights; a combined permit with no text of its own is judged on its project text. (4) Why: the AI read 66% of the old group as routine maintenance and agreed with the group on 1.3% (`docs/reports/permit-intent-agreement.md`), and the top 20 leads were almost all "Electrical, plumbing and mechanical work approved". Effects are in `docs/reports/presale-reclass.md`. (5) The rules are deterministic regexes, not AI; AI permit intent remains shadow data. Known limit: a permit whose description is blank and whose project has no text is treated as maintenance, which may under-count real prep work.
- **D15** (2026-11-20, Phase 5H) MEP permits for ADU work are ADU permits. An electrical / plumbing / mechanical permit whose text names an ADU or accessory dwelling, a conversion into units, or new attached/detached units goes to the `adu` group (`adu_scope` in `presale_rules`, checked before `cosmetic_scope`), with the same weight as other ADU permits (10), not `pre_sale_prep` (20) and not `maintenance`. Scoring model v0.4.1: no weights changed. Why: 1505 Felspar St's mechanical, electrical and plumbing permits were "work associated with the conversion of storages and garages into (5) new attached ADUs" and were counted as pre-sale prep. The top signal line on every list and lead page is now the signal with the highest score contribution (zero weight never shown, ties go to the most recent).
- **D16** (2026-11-20, Phase 5H) Cloud guard: every pipeline entry point refuses a non-local database host unless `--cloud` is passed (`territory/db.py`).
- **D17** (2026-11-21, Phase 5I) Score calibration, model `v0.5-handset`. (1) **Problem:** the best multi-signal leads were compressed into 47-63 (top lead 63, median parcel 15), so "Hot" (80+) was empty and a 4-family lead barely outranked a 2-family one. (2) **Change:** stacking bonus +15% per independent family beyond the first, capped at +45% (was +10% / +30%), and base weights raised so evidence strength shows before stacking: tax default 30 to 40, STR removed 25 to 40, permits pre-sale 20 to 36, demolition/lot split 15 to 27, ADU 10 to 18, remodel/addition 8 to 14, stalled 15 to 27, SB 79 14/14/10 to 21/21/15, SB 9 8 to 14, fire 10/6/3 to 15/9/4, owner modifiers about +30% (tenure 15/10 to 20/13, absentee 10 to 13, out-of-state 12 to 15, no mortgage 10 to 13, equity 5 to 6, owner cap 30 to 40). Context-only and low-intent weights did not change (STR active 5, solar 3, maintenance and other 0). Half-lives, confidence, the residential filter and suppression are unchanged. (3) **Chosen by simulation** over the stored score components (a weight scales its component linearly), not by trial rescoring, because scores are append-only. (4) **Result, local database, 26,268 scored parcels:** 0-9: 6253, 10-19: 6937, 20-29: 6402, 30-39: 4175, 40-49: 1858, 50-59: 484, 60-69: 128, 70-79: 25, 80-89: 5, 90-99: 1. Top 20 leads now score about 74-91 (were 47-63). A parcel with one signal family never exceeds 40; the long tail below 30 is unchanged in shape, so low-intent noise is not inflated. (5) **Limit:** only 31 parcels reach 70+, because the owner data is still sample data and few parcels carry three or four independent families; the spread will widen when ATTOM owner data (D9) replaces the sample. (6) The Active leads tile shows "Scoring updated" instead of a percentage while the model differs between this week and the comparison week (`scoring_model_weeks()`), so the rescore does not read as a collapse or surge.
- **D18** (2026-11-21, Phase 5I) Presentation fixes before the demo. (1) **AI telemetry is system status, not sales information.** Token counts and API cost moved from /pipeline to a "System & AI pipeline status" section at the bottom of /data-sources; /pipeline is a sales progression (Flagged, Contacted, Conversation, Appointment, Listing) with step-to-step conversion and estimated GCI at 2.5% of the owner's estimated value (labeled "est." and "Sample" when the value is sample owner data; summed over distinct parcels that reached each stage, using the latest owner row). (2) **Recipe cards show the recipe's own fact**, not the parcel's global top signal: each recipe has a fixed list of qualifying components in `present.ts` (`recipeSignalLine`); a parcel with none of them shows no line instead of an unrelated one, and tax default and ownership transfer are never shown. (3) **Trends permit chart and takeaway count only actionable groups** (remodel/addition, ADU, pre-sale prep, demolition/lot split, solar); routine systems work and other permits sit behind "Include routine & other permits".
